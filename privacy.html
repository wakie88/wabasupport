Privacy Policy

Wabacrobes: ALife Wallpaper • Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: Wabacrobes does not collect, record, process, transmit, store, or sell any personal data whatsoever. It does not require internet permissions and runs 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Wabacrobes is a local procedural live wallpaper simulation. We strictly collect zero user data:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Device Information or Identifiers: No Android Advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address, IP address, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Location Data: Zero GPS, network-based location, or Wi-Fi beacon access.
  • No Usage or Behavioral Analytics: No tracking SDKs, no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, and no telemetry of any kind.

2. App Permissions

Wabacrobes requires only the bare minimum operating system permissions necessary to function as an Android live wallpaper:

  • android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER: Allows setting the wallpaper directly from the application preview activity.
  • android.permission.BIND_WALLPAPER: Android standard service declaration enabling the OS Wallpaper Manager to render the service.

Wabacrobes does NOT request android.permission.INTERNET. It is physically incapable of transmitting data over any network.

3. Third-Party Libraries & Advertisements

Wabacrobes contains zero third-party advertisement networks and zero closed-source tracking SDKs. It is completely ad-free and tracking-free.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because Wabacrobes collects absolutely no data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy. It is safe for users of all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Should any updates be made to this Privacy Policy in the future, the revised document will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
